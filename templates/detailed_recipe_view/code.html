Creamy Tomato Pasta

Ingredients

1 lb pasta

1 cup heavy cream

1 can crushed tomatoes

1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

2 cloves garlic, minced

2 tbsp olive oil

Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

Step 1

Cook pasta according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.

Step 2

Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 3

Pour in crushed tomatoes and bring to a simmer. Season with salt and pepper.

Step 4

Stir in heavy cream and Parmesan cheese. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.

Step 5

Add cooked pasta to the sauce and toss to coat evenly. Serve immediately.
